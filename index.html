About

Ray Waweru is an author and Computer Science student at Kibabii University whose writing sits at the intersection of human psychology, personal power, and self-mastery.

He is the author of We Are All Prisoners and The Authority Within, works that established him as a thoughtful and perceptive voice on the limitations people place on themselves and the freedom that lies beyond them.

In The Authority Within, Ray brings the same intellectual depth and clarity to the subject of confidence, delivering a work that is both rigorously honest and profoundly practical. Ray Waweru writes for those who are ready to make the decision to change.
